# Value: the honest answers (2026-11-07)

## Why would anyone pay for this today?
**Honestly, almost nobody would.** Cooked+ today is a Daily archive, two spreads and a badge on a game people
call boring. Nothing about it hurts enough to fix, and nothing is worth showing off. The research is clear
that game payers mostly pay for one of four things (docs/research.md §6):
1. **Identity and status:** a character or look that says something about them and that friends see.
2. **More of something they already love:** modes, levels, a season of new content.
3. **Convenience:** no ads, skip a wait, keep a streak.
4. **Supporting a creator they like:** real, but small.

We had none of the first three in a form worth paying for.

## What problem does it solve for a Gen Z player?
Not a "focus" problem. The research says practice doesn't improve real-life attention, and we won't claim it
does. The honest job-to-be-done is **social entertainment in under a minute**:
- **A quick "am I cooked?" self-check** in the slang they already use, with a result that's funny to post.
- **Bragging and roasting:** "I beat you, you're cooked", sent to a group chat.
- **A shared daily ritual:** the same challenge as everyone else, compared without spoilers (the Wordle effect).

## What do we give that free games on their phone don't?
Today: **nothing that holds up.** Free games have more content and better production. Where we *can* win:
1. **Zero install from a TikTok or Instagram link.** You tap and you're playing in about 1.5 seconds. Store games can't do that.
2. **Same-rounds duels by link.** "Beat my 23" sends your friend the exact same sequence. It's fair, instant and works in any group chat.
3. **A daily shared seed with a spoiler-free share grid.** That's a habit loop people already know from Wordle.
4. **Meme-native identity:** results and characters that look and sound like Gen Z internet, not a children's app.

## What changes it (the plan this pass builds)
- **Modes that feel different, not just faster:** Blitz (60-second score attack with combos), Classic (lives and rising speed),
  Chill (no fail, practice), Hardcore (one life, ranked), Daily, and Duel.
- **A reason to keep playing:** XP and levels, daily missions, a fixed daily reward with no gambling, streaks, and daily and
  weekly leaderboards.
- **A collectible character roster** players unlock by playing or buy outright. These are things people want to be seen with.
- **A pass worth buying:** a season of new characters plus extra modes. A free track keeps non-payers progressing.
- **Price structure:** a cheap first purchase, cosmetics at fixed real-money prices, and no loot boxes or random paid rewards.

## What would prove us wrong
If day-1 retention stays under about 25% and fewer than 2% of players share a result, the core game isn't fun enough
yet, and no monetization will fix that. We'll watch those two numbers before spending on content.
